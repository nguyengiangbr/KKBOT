# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the KKBOT project a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- **Be respectful** – treat others with courtesy and kindness.
- **Be collaborative** – welcome diverse viewpoints and work constructively.
- **Be inclusive** – foster an environment where anyone can contribute.
- **No harassment** – any form of harassment, discrimination, or intimidation is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at `maintainers@kkbot.dev`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
